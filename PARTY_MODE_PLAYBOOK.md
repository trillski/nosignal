# NO SIGNAL: 11:59 — Case 01, Party Mode
**Version:** 1.0 | **Audience:** 1–6 friends, one-device pass-and-play | **Length:** 10–18 minutes (flexible)

## Product definition
NO SIGNAL is a cooperative mystery about a forged midnight confession. Six investigators hold different pieces of evidence. The group has to connect what happened to Camera 08, a copied staff badge, inflated donation numbers, Mara's real recording, and the person who queued the forgery.

Party Mode is **not** a forced drinking game. It is easy to play at a casual or drinking game night without requiring drinking, timed punishments, or roleplay performance. It uses a different interface than the original seven-lock Classic Mode, which remains available.

One licensed customer hosts the same-device Party Mode and can pass the phone between one to six friends. Every round contains six short role files; with fewer than six people, participants handle more than one role. Party Mode can also be downloaded as a single HTML file that runs offline.

## Quick start (20 seconds)
1. Pick 1–6 players. Five rounds, about 10–18 minutes; no account setup.
2. Pass the phone. Each player opens a private clue, memorizes one fact, then taps **Hide Clue + Pass Device**.
3. Compare the evidence out loud.
4. As a group, tap one of two actions. See the result, move to the next round.
5. After Round 5, identify who queued the fake confession and choose what to do with the broadcast. Reveal your ending.

Players do not need to solve arithmetic codes or study long dossiers. Optional 45-second discussion countdown never blocks progress.

## Full rewritten Case 01 flow

### Round 1 — The Blackout (11:47 PM)
**Premise:** Camera 08 went dark just before a false confession was scheduled.

| Role | Private fact | Shareable takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Engineer | Camera was switched off | The blackout was deliberate |
| Security Lead | Camera 08 is on Floor 04 | Look at the service floor |
| Stage Manager | The blackout cue was 17 | The cue may matter |
| Finance Auditor | Donation totals weren't approved | Something else was hidden |
| Reporter | Mara said not to trust the broadcast | The video could be a setup |
| Network Analyst | A valid device cut the signal | Someone used system access |

**Group choice:** Restore Camera 08 **or** trace the relay. **Revealed consequence:** The interruption was deliberate, and the corridor was used during the blind spot. Advance automatically.

### Round 2 — The Impossible Badge (11:49 PM)
**Premise:** Eli's badge appears somewhere Eli couldn't have been.

| Role | Private fact | Shareable takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | Badge ping crossed broadcast network | Not a normal door scan |
| Security | Eli appears in lobby at 11:49 | He can't be in two places |
| Stage | MER-02 was active | Producer suite is suspicious |
| Finance | Eli carried charity envelopes | His movements have an explanation |
| Reporter | I saw Eli in the lobby | A badge isn't proof of presence |
| Network | Identical badge appeared twice | One scan was copied |

**Group choice:** Follow badge **or** check computer. **Revealed consequence:** The badge was copied; MER-02 accepted the forged credential. Advance.

### Round 3 — The Fake Total (11:51 PM)
**Premise:** On-air donations do not match verified receipts.

| Role | Private fact | Shareable takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | On-screen total $284,950 | The larger number was broadcast |
| Security | No envelopes left the lobby | Missing cash isn't proven |
| Stage | Host was told to wait | Number wasn't cleared |
| Finance | Verified receipts $248,950 | Public number is wrong |
| Reporter | Mara challenged that number | She wanted the truth shown |
| Network | MER-02 edited the ticker | Change was deliberate |

**Group choice:** Compare numbers **or** inspect donations. **Revealed consequence:** $36,000 was inflated on screen; this does **not** establish theft. Advance.

### Round 4 — The Fake Confession (11:55 PM)
**Premise:** Two audio files claim to be Mara's confession.

| Role | Private fact | Shareable takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | Forged apology has no bells | Its sound was altered |
| Security | Blue door S3 opened at 11:52 | Someone used the corridor |
| Stage | Three bells rang at 11:46 | Real clip has bell audio |
| Finance | Mara demanded clean ledger | She exposed false accounting |
| Reporter | Real Mara said: “Numbers cannot forgive a lie.” | True audio contains that phrase |
| Network | MASTER-2346 predates apology | The confession was uploaded later |

**Group choice:** Find Mara **or** check audio. **Revealed consequence:** Mara is safe behind S3 with the authentic original recording. Advance.

### Round 5 — Who Pressed Send? (11:57 PM)
**Premise:** The forgery was queued for the midnight broadcast.

| Role | Private fact | Shareable takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | Q-1159 set to go live at midnight | Time is running out |
| Security | Eli loaned Ren his badge | Badge was borrowed |
| Stage | Joy was onstage | She didn't use MER-02 |
| Finance | Evelyn approved inflated total | This alone doesn't prove she ordered forgery |
| Reporter | Ren ended Mara's interview | He wanted her silenced |
| Network | Job on MER-02 signed REN | The uploader was Ren |

**Group choice:** Suspect Ren **or** suspect Eli. **Revealed consequence:** The signed production job identifies Ren as operator, but the person who ordered it remains unproven. Advance.

### Final broadcast decision (11:59 PM)
The group makes **two** choices: *Who queued the forgery?* (Ren/Eli) and *What should happen?* (air authentic proof/preserve and wait/cut the feed). The game resolves the ending:

| Choice | Ending |
|---|---|
| Correctly identify Ren + air authentic proof | **The Truth Goes Live** |
| Correctly identify Ren + preserve proof and wait | **Evidence Saved** |
| Correctly identify Ren + cut the feed | **The Signal Goes Dark** |
| Accuse Eli instead | **The Wrong Accusation** |

This version emphasizes social judgment, not a perfect score. No gore, punishments, drinking requirements, or real-life crime claims.

## Game-design requirements
- Most screens contain only one fact, one action, or two choices.
- Private clue: one fact + one short takeaway. No paragraphs.
- Role names stay distinct and correct across every round.
- Large touch controls (minimum ~56px tall); designed for portrait phones.
- Player-specific clue is hidden with one tap to permit private sharing.
- Two clear group actions, consequence immediately revealed.
- Optional timer; no forced timer fail state, penalties, or drinking requirement.
- Host can play alone or pass the same phone among up to six people.
- Full progress is saved in local storage when possible; Replay restarts.
- Reader-friendly color contrast, keyboard focus outline, reduced-motion support.
- The Classic Mode with seven more complex puzzles remains available separately.

## Video production rules based on the owner's example
The screenshot attached by the owner showed a headline covering the game title, many role tabs, and a full dossier with tiny body text. Do **not** show that whole interface in advertisements.
- **0–2 s**: understandable contradiction, e.g. badge used despite an alibi.
- **2–6 s**: single visual clue, not multiple unreadable paragraphs.
- **6–10 s**: show a character card tapped, then another friend's contradictory clue.
- **10–13 s**: visible A/B decision and consequence; never crop labels.
- **13–16 s**: user's exact approved CRT/glitch NO SIGNAL badge; one CTA.
- Keep text overlays outside click targets and clue text. No surprise shaking or mismatched footage.
- Build script/shotboard first, render with available tools, then inspect at least 5 frames and the entire timed cut with audio. Fix misalignments before publication.
- Show **real game interactions** where claimed. Fictional dramatization must not masquerade as genuine buyers.

## Quality assurance
Automated browser QA should exercise: role reveal and hide, all six roles in all five rounds, the A/B decision on each round, all four endings, saved progress, and restart. Separate payment QA must confirm an unauthorized visitor cannot read \`/party\` or download \`/download-party\`, while buyers can access both. An unpaid simulated Stripe session is not equivalent to a real purchase.
